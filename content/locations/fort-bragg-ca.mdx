summary: We stayed coastal in September, heading a few hours south of Bandon into the Mendocino area of Northern California. Fort Bragg is small, primarily industrial town that sits along Highway 1 at the coastal edge of a temperate rainforest. The town has a pretty small footprint and just steps outside the street grid puts you among 200 foot tall old growth redwoods in a canopy of lush green vines and blackberry patches.
overview: Fort Bragg's coastal location and proximity to beautiful outdoor spaces attracts some tourism but it's fairly isolated and the town seems to be pretty self-sufficient and keep themselves busy and entertained. There is regularly live music at restaurants, not big productions just one or two people with a mic and guitar providing ambiance.

    - In true small town fashion, [Drop In Donut](https://www.dropindonut.com/) is only open a few days a week and close once they sell all their donuts for the day. But on Friday evenings they reopen and sell hand-dipped corn dogs. Yes, I absolutely had a donut and a corn dog for dinner last week.
    - The weekly [Farmers Market](https://mcfarm.org/farmers-markets/fort-bragg-farmers-market) has a delightful selection of fruits and veggies, flowers, breads, preserves, and much more with a live musician to set the mood while you peruse.
    - [La Palapa](https://lapalapaca.com/) has delicious $2 tacos on Tuesdays.
    - [Tall Guy Brewing](https://tallguybrewing.beer/) has pretty good beer and rotating popup kitchens. Renegades of Pho is in house on Sundays and Mondays and is simply outstanding!
