overview: |
  Our schedule for the second half of July was dictated by a work twofer. Erik attended Ruby Conf at the Red Rock Casino in Vegas and my company hosted a get together in San Diego the following week. So we decided July would be the month of interstitial stops.  

  After a couple weeks in Boise, we packed up Black Betty, the minivan, and headed south. Driving from Boise to Vegas is, by and large, pretty boring with the exception of Southern Utah with its beautiful red stone and rugged mountain skylines. I am of the mind that road trips are a lot more fun when you can break up the drive with interesting stops. This trip we ate at a diner next to a bowling alley, saw an incredible art exhibit, and attended a Shakespeare play. Like, so cultured.
