    - Erik is in love with [Bandon Dunes](https://bandondunesgolf.com/). There are seven golf courses crafted in the Scottish links style. The Scots are golf OGs and consider sandy coasts to be premier golfing locations. This wind though...
    - Gemini Lounge looks like a murder shack on the outside and is fully painted red on the inside. But they have fantastic cocktails, kind bar staff, and live music.
    - [Old Town Tap & Grill](https://www.oldtowntapandgrill.com/) is dark and a little weird but has good beer and live music on Saturdays.
    - [Circles in the Sand](https://www.sandypathbandon.com/) is an organization that creates intricate sand labyrinths. People can walk the maze until the tide comes in.
